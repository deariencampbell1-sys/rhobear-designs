Recreation trade-offs — Grok Composer 2.5

  • Imagery: All photos, case-study screenshots, and team portraits replaced with CSS gradient placeholders — no external brand assets loaded.
  • Hero transitions: Original uses rapid Webflow/AVIF image cycling; approximated with GSAP crossfade on gradient slides rather than pixel-perfect video frames.
  • Typography: Inter substitutes the site's custom grotesk; weight and scale tuned to match editorial boldness but exact kerning differs.
  • Interactions: Scroll-linked parallax, cursor effects, and CMS-driven case-study pages omitted for single-file portability.
  • Service counters: Original shows incrementing (00)→(01)→(02)→(03); static (00) labels used here with section order conveying sequence.
  • Localization: FR toggle is presentational only; no bilingual content swap implemented.
