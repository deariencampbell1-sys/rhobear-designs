— · studio · 01

We are a small studio of designers and engineers who think the browser is the most interesting surface in computing. We do not make templates. We do not make slides. We make things that load in a tab and feel like they should not.

Most of our work is collaborative. We sit with the team that hired us, we drink their coffee, we learn their problem. Then we build the smallest possible thing that proves the idea — and we keep going until the idea is obvious.

The work is technical and the team is technical. We hire people who can read a shader, write a press release, and tell the difference.

— the studio active theory
