01 hello from wellington

We make the
internet feel alive
and a little weird.

Resn is a small interactive studio in Aotearoa New Zealand. We design and build playful, story-driven digital work for brands that want to be remembered, not just seen.

02 why we make

We make things that earn the next click. Then the next. Then the next.

Most of the web is wallpaper now. We would rather build a small, alive corner of it that someone tells a friend about on a Tuesday afternoon. That is the whole job — make the kind of thing that gets shared because it was enjoyed.

04 the studio

Twenty people. One workshop. No silos.

Resn was founded in 2004 above a record store on Cuba Street. Today we are still a single room of designers, illustrators and engineers, working shoulder to shoulder on a small number of projects at a time.

We take on four to five projects a year. We say no to most of what comes in. The ones we say yes to, we care about like they're ours — because they are, by the end.
