I
chapter one · prologue

First, a quiet room.

The story begins in a room that is not entirely dark. There is a desk, a single warm lamp, and a screen showing a city that is not entirely real. The cursor blinks. Someone has been here for a long time.

He calls himself giulio. He makes things that move. He makes things that move slowly, on purpose, because he believes the slow ones are the ones that stay with you.

II
chapter two · origin

How does a person become a person?

He grew up in a town where nothing ever happened and so he made things happen, on a small screen, with patience. He learned to draw, then to code, then to draw with code. He stopped apologising for liking both.

He built his first interactive site in 2011 — a black page with a single white square that followed the cursor. Two thousand people opened it. None of them were family. He considers it his first real audience.

III
chapter three · studio

A small studio. A long name.

He runs a studio of three. The studio is called they call me giulio because that is the only line that ever made every client smile. The work is mostly interactive narrative for fashion, film and the occasional museum.

The studio refuses projects that can be done by a tool. They look for the parts that need a human hand and a slightly unhealthy attention to timing.

IV
chapter four · signal

The work is not the point.

The work is the receipt. The point is the conversation you have with a stranger at 2 a.m. who tells you that a thing you made made them feel something they did not have a word for. That is the whole reason.

If you have a project that needs that kind of conversation — send a note. He reads every email. He replies to the ones that make him feel something. He replies slowly, but he replies.
